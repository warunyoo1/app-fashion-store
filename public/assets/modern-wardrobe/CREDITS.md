# Modern wardrobe renders

These 22 thumbnails depict the actual RACK garment assemblies in their two new colorways. Source GLBs and texture files are retained unchanged. For this presentation, allowlisted clothing materials have solid-color diffuse finishes and adjusted roughness; original geometry, normal/AO maps and hardware are retained. Product names and colorway names are demonstration styling, not the original creators' product names.

- Studio Tee, Essential Hoodie, Leather Jacket and Camp Collar Shirt use [Clothes by Naira](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/clothes-54646263f96a47be9b3d2befe8d0555e).
- Tie-detail Shirt, Utility Jacket, Cropped Leather Jacket, Panel Hoodie, Relaxed Hoodie and Soft Hoodie use the Style3D CG assets linked individually in [the source ledger](../sources.json).
- Boxy Tee uses the Obridje asset linked in [the source ledger](../sources.json).
- Wooden hangers use [CC0 - Shirt On Hanger by plaggy](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cc0-shirt-on-hanger-846d8f285fcb426498033ae67aee2d10).

All source models are used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). These images are adapted material presentations by RACK. Exact source titles, source URLs and earlier geometry modifications remain in public/assets/sources.json and each source model's CREDITS file. The Relaxed Tee by funlab117 keeps its existing Chalk/Ink renders and attribution.
